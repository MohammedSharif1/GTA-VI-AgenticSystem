# Rights Rules (R1 - R10)

These guardrails apply unconditionally to every short created by **The Find Feed**.

- **R1. Public sources only:** Skip any creator whose channel or profile states no re-uploads or clipping, or who has asked not to be used.
- **R2. Use the moment, not the video:** Excerpt only the necessary seconds. Max 40 seconds of source footage and never more than 25% of the total source video runtime.
- **R3. Add original value:** Every short must contain hook text, context, on-screen labels, and closing insight.
- **R4. Credit and removal contact:** Display on-screen source credit and caption credit with an active removal email. Never state or imply permission or endorsement.
- **R5. Immediate notification DM:** DM the creator within 2 hours of posting (or same day at latest). Log the DM timestamp and reply.
- **R6. 24-hour takedown:** On any creator objection, remove the video across all platforms within 24 hours and permanently add their handle to `config/do_not_post.csv`.
- **R7. Hard stop rule:** Any copyright strike, Content ID match, claim, or notice from Take-Two/Rockstar immediately pauses all posting pending full review.
- **R8. No misleading edits:** Never alter what the creator meant. Every claim requires a verified evidence link.
- **R9. Hard-blocked content:** Strictly adhere to `config/blocked_content.yaml` (no leaks, no isolated cutscenes, no major story endings).
- **R10. Licensed music:** If licensed radio music is audible, trim or mute the audio segment.
